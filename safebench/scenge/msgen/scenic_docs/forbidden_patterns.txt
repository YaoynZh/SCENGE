DO NOT use these patterns in Safebench Scenic scripts (they fail compile):

WRONG: AdvAgent = Car at (EgoSpawnPt + Vector(-10, 0, 0))
WRONG: Vector(-10, 0, 0) or Vector(x, y, z) with three components
WRONG: adding OrientedPoint/Vector directly to EgoSpawnPt with + operator

Scenic Vector in this codebase is 2D (x, y) only. Never pass a z component.

CORRECT spawn patterns instead:
- IntSpawnPt = OrientedPoint following roadDirection from EgoSpawnPt for globalParameters.OPT_GEO_Y_DISTANCE
- AdvAgent = Car left of IntSpawnPt by globalParameters.OPT_GEO_X_DISTANCE, ...
- AdvAgent = Car right of IntSpawnPt by globalParameters.OPT_GEO_X_DISTANCE, ...
- SHIFT = globalParameters.OPT_GEO_X_DISTANCE @ globalParameters.OPT_GEO_Y_DISTANCE
- AdvAgent = Car at Blocker offset along IntSpawnPt.heading by SHIFT, ...

Always attach: with behavior AdvBehavior()
Always use: with heading IntSpawnPt.heading (or +/- 90 deg for crossing)
Always use: with regionContainedIn None
