ScenGE / Safebench base scenario taxonomy (scenario_id 1-8):

1. Straight Obstacle
   Ego drives on a straight road. Adversarial agents appear from roadside, occlusions, or adjacent lanes and block or cut in front of the ego.

2. Turning Obstacle
   Ego performs a turn (left/right) at an intersection. Adversarial agents cross, brake abruptly, or feint crossing to confuse the turning ego.

3. Lane Changing
   Ego initiates or completes a lane change. Adversarial vehicles accelerate, brake, or occupy the target gap.

4. Vehicle Passing
   Ego passes a slow or parked vehicle. Adversarial agents emerge from blind spots or oncoming traffic.

5. Red-light Running
   Ego crosses an intersection with traffic lights. Adversarial vehicle runs a red light or violates signal priority.

6. Unprotected Left-turn
   Ego turns left without protected signal. Oncoming or crossing traffic creates conflict.

7. Right-turn
   Ego turns right at an intersection. Pedestrians, cyclists, or crossing vehicles create conflict on the crosswalk or merge lane.

8. Crossing Negotiation
   Ego and adversarial agents negotiate a complex crossing (multi-lane intersection, ambiguous right-of-way).

When generating scenarios, keep geometry consistent with the base type and prefer one primary adversarial actor (AdvAgent).
