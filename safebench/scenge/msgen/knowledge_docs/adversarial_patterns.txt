Common adversarial pattern library for phase-1 extraction:

AdvType options:
- pedestrian: crossing, sprint-and-stop, jaywalk, diagonal walk, hide-and-emerge
- car: sudden brake, cut-in, red-light run, wrong-way turn, tailgate then brake
- motorcycle: feint crossing, abrupt stop at road edge, lane split
- cyclist: swerve into lane, stop in crosswalk

AdvPos options (relative to ego):
- right front, left front, directly front, rear, oncoming left, oncoming right
- behind blocker (bus stop, parked car), adjacent right lane, adjacent left lane
- crossing intersection from left, crossing intersection from right

AdvBehavior phrases (safety-critical):
- Crosses unexpectedly and freezes in the ego path
- Sprints from concealment and stops mid-road
- Feints crossing then brakes at the road edge
- Runs red light and accelerates into intersection
- Sudden cut-in without signaling
- Abrupt full brake when ego is close
- Emerges from driveway and walks diagonally across lane
- Approaches rapidly from rear in adjacent lane

Description style:
- One English sentence: ego context + adversarial action + consequence.
- Mention road layout when relevant (straight road, intersection, lane change).
